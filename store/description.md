# Harbour store listing

Title: Popquizza
Categories: Games / Trivia

## Short summary

Ten pop music questions, every day.

## Description

How well do you know the charts? Popquizza serves up ten new
multiple-choice questions each day, drawn from a classic week in UK
chart history — who was No 1, who was climbing, and who you've
completely forgotten about.

- A fresh quiz every midnight
- Build a daily streak and track your average
- Full history of every quiz you've played
- Share your score (and your ✅❌ line) with friends
- Native Sailfish app: swipe to submit, works with your ambience

Questions are fetched from popquizza.com; your scores and streaks stay
on your phone. Also playable on the web at popquizza.com. Free
software (AGPL).
